# Source and citation audit

Verified November 19, 2026. Local revision only.

The audit checked (1) original publication identity and bibliographic metadata, (2) whether cited passages support the website’s actual claims, and (3) citation-to-reference consistency in static HTML, interactive panels, the methods page, and the presentation guide. Three independent reviews covered legal sources, government reports, and academic works; the main editor reconciled the results.

## Result

All 11 referenced works are real. Nine are official government publications: eight contemporary primary records plus the State Department’s retrospective historical overview. The remaining works are Hristova’s peer-reviewed journal article and D’Ignazio/Klein’s academic book. Government provenance does not make a document an independent account of lived Iraqi experience, and the book is not being substituted for the required peer-reviewed article.

## Corrections made

- GAO-08-1065 is dated **September 26, 2008**, following its canonical publication record and dated report letter. Its older accessible-text header says September 29; the canonical record is preferred.
- The 2014 Section 215 legal objection and recommendation to end the program reflect a **3–2 board majority**, not unanimity or a court ruling.
- Metadata is described as phone numbers, timing, and duration, rather than identified callers or conversation contents.
- Section 702’s targeting authority and incidental collection of Americans’ communications now cite separate documents.
- The 2015 law and the implementation fact sheet have separate entries; the transition date is sourced to the dated November 27, 2015 fact sheet.
- The State Department overview is identified as a retrospective secondary reference.
- Combined entries were split into one reference per document, and page/section locators were added.

## Citation method

The website and presentation guide use APA 7 author–date citations and an alphabetical reference list. Reports use publication years; works by the same author in the same year use a/b suffixes in title order: the GAO reports are 2008a (DOD can establish) and 2008b (DOD needs to establish), and the board reports are 2014a (surveillance program) and 2014b (telephone records program). Printed page numbers use p./pp.; statutes use §/§§; web material uses section titles or book chapters. Statutes follow APA legal-reference conventions, with the act title, public-law number, Statutes at Large citation, and year. Article and book references use DOI links. The source-by-source notes below preserve full publication dates and original titles as verification metadata; they are not a separate citation style.

## Academic-source checks and access limits

Hristova’s original PDF and the publisher’s issue contents agree on **12(4), 516–527 (2014)** and DOI **10.24908/ss.v12i4.5045**. The article is in the journal’s Articles section. The official journal policy describes double-blind review by two reviewers for research Articles. The article landing page gives September 23, while the issue page gives November 25; only the unambiguous year 2014 is cited. No correction or retraction notice was visible on the article record or targeted publisher searches; that is not a guarantee against future notices.

- [Article record](https://ojs.library.queensu.ca/index.php/surveillance-and-society/article/view/friendfoe)
- [Issue contents](https://ojs.library.queensu.ca/index.php/surveillance-and-society/issue/view/515)
- [Official peer-review policy](https://ojs.library.queensu.ca/index.php/surveillance-and-society/about)

The policy page triggered a redirect loop in direct web retrieval; its official search-indexed policy text was available. The article PDF and issue contents were directly accessible. The scholarly source’s identity, pagination, DOI, and relevant argument were checked against the original PDF, not the search summary.

Data Feminism is cited as the original 2020 edition, not the later paperback. Relevant original-book chapters are [6: Consider Context](https://data-feminism.mitpress.mit.edu/pub/czq9dfs5) and [7: Make Labor Visible](https://data-feminism.mitpress.mit.edu/pub/0vgzaln4).

PCLOB’s [official oversight index](https://www.pclob.gov/Oversight) independently lists both 2014 reports. The 2022 date in the Section 702 PDF filename is an upload/accessibility filename, not the report’s publication date. GovInfo’s automatically generated citation for the Section 215 report uses January 1; the original report cover gives January 23, 2014, which is the date retained here.

The GAO PDF screenshot tool returned a cache error during one review. Original extracted report text and canonical metadata remained available; no visual PDF verification is claimed for that failed screenshot.

The audit is historical (2001–2015), not a check of today’s surveillance-law status. The student still needs to confirm that Hristova is not a required class reading, and document actual human involvement and the final REAL rating.
